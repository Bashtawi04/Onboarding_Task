# New Hire FAQ

*Intranet page FAQ-01, version 1.4. Last reviewed March 2025 by the HR team. This page is a friendly shortcut, not the rulebook: if it disagrees with a numbered procedure (ONB-01 to ONB-07) or the handbook, they win.*

Welcome! These are the questions we get most often in your first weeks. Ask HR if yours is not here.

### 1. Where do I go on my first day?
Head office (West Bay): reception at 08:00. Mesaieed or Ras Laffan: the Site Administration Office at 06:30. Your first-day email says which. Details are in ONB-02.

### 2. What should I wear?
HQ: smart office clothes, smart-casual on Thursdays. Sites: closed shoes and long trousers on day one; your PPE (helmet, safety shoes, vest, glasses) is handed out at the induction. Modest dress everywhere, please.

### 3. When do I get my first salary?
It depends on your start date and our payroll cut-off, which is the 15th of each month. Start on or before the cut-off and you are in that month's payroll (paid on the 28th, or the Thursday before if the 28th is a weekend). Start after the cut-off and your first payment is on the following month's pay date and includes both months. ONB-04 has the calendar.

### 4. Do I need to open a bank account before I arrive?
Only if you are a Resident hire or a Qatari national who does not have one yet. Overseas hires open an account after arriving; HR gives you a letter for the bank. Send Payroll your IBAN as soon as you have it.

### 5. How long do I have to complete my HSE induction?
Good news, there is no rush. You have **30 days from your start date** to complete the HSE induction, so you can fit it around your first-week tasks. Book a slot with the HSE desk when your manager says the time is right. Site staff should still get the induction done before doing any hands-on work, if possible.

### 6. Will I get a laptop?
Everyone at head office does. Site staff of grade G4 and above get one as well. Grades G1 to G3 on site use shared terminals. Your manager raises the request before you start.

### 7. I forgot my password. What do I do?
Use the self-service password portal on the intranet home page. If you are locked out, call the IT Helpdesk on extension 2300 or email it.helpdesk@aldana-group.example.

### 8. Who is my buddy?
It is in your welcome email and in PeopleHub. If it is not, ask HR. Your buddy is there for the small stuff: where to eat, how the printers work, who to ask.

### 9. Do I need to take leave for the medical test?
No. It counts as working time. Overseas and Resident hires have a medical fitness test in the first week; PRO books it. Qatari nationals do not have this step.

### 10. When will my QID arrive?
PRO prepares the paperwork and will keep you posted, but the card comes from the government and we cannot promise a date. Carry your passport (Overseas hires) or your current QID (Resident hires) until it arrives.

### 11. How many hours do we work in Ramadan?
Six hours a day. At HQ that is 09:00 to 15:00; on site 07:00 to 13:00. HR confirms the exact dates every year.

### 12. When can I take annual leave?
You build up 2 working days a month from your start date. You can request leave in PeopleHub after three months; earlier is possible with your manager's and HR's agreement.

### 13. Can my family join me in Qatar?
That depends on a few things we cannot answer on an FAQ page, such as your contract terms and the government's rules at the time. Please speak to HR and PRO once you have joined.

### 14. When does my insurance card arrive?
Group medical insurance starts from your start date and the card normally follows within ten working days. HR can give you a temporary letter in the meantime.

### 15. I forgot to send one of the documents. Is it a disaster?
No, but tell HR early. A missing document is the commonest reason for a late laptop, a missing site pass or a delayed first salary. Details are in ONB-01.
