## 1. Your account

IT creates your Al Dana account and email address **one working day before your start date**. IT can only do this after HR has confirmed that your contract is signed, so sign it on time (ONB-01). Your email address follows the pattern `firstname.lastname@aldana-group.example`.

On day one IT gives you your temporary sign-in details in person. At your first sign-in you set your own password and set up **multi-factor authentication** (MFA, a second check such as a code on your phone). MFA is mandatory for everybody.

## 2. Laptop and equipment: office versus site

Your line manager raises an IT request **at least 3 working days before your start date**. Without it IT cannot prepare your device.

| Where you work | What you get | Where you collect it |
|---|---|---|
| **Doha HQ**, any grade | Laptop on day one | IT room at HQ (third floor), after your welcome with HR |
| **Mesaieed Site or Ras Laffan Site, grade G4 and above** | Laptop, with VPN access set up | **The IT desk on your own site**, after your HSE induction is signed off (Mesaieed ext 3300, Ras Laffan ext 4300) |
| **Mesaieed Site or Ras Laffan Site, grades G1 to G3** | No laptop. A shared site terminal and the PeopleHub kiosk; your supervisor shows you how to log in | Nothing to collect |

Site staff never need to travel to HQ to collect equipment. Extra items (second screen, headset) are requested through the IT Helpdesk after your first week, with your manager's approval.

## 3. Passwords and sign-in problems

- Forgotten password: use the self-service password portal (link on the intranet home page).
- Locked out, new phone, or MFA not working: call the IT Helpdesk on extension **2300** (or the site IT desk).
- Do not share your password with anybody, **including IT**. IT will never ask for it.
- Lost or stolen laptop, phone or ID card: report to the IT Helpdesk the same day.
