## 1. How probation works at Al Dana

Probation lasts **six months** from your start date. This is the longest probation the Labour Law allows, and we do not extend it: the decision is made inside the six months. During probation you have the same working hours, safety rules and benefits as everybody else. Leave can be requested after three months (see the handbook).

## 2. The first week: objectives

Within **five working days** of your start date, your manager agrees **three to five probation objectives** with you and records them in PeopleHub. This is the task `PROBATION_PLAN`, owned by the manager. Good objectives are specific ("complete the HSE induction and the pump-maintenance course by day 60") rather than general ("learn the job"). If you have not seen them by the end of week one, ask your manager, then HR.

## 3. Your buddy

Your buddy is a colleague who has been with us for some time. They are not your boss and have no say in your probation result. Expect a short weekly chat in the first month, and a lot of "where do I find..." questions answered. Your buddy's name is in your welcome email and in your HR record.

## 4. Check-ins at day 30, 60 and 90

Your manager meets you for about 30 minutes at day 30, day 60 and day 90. Cover three things: what is going well, what is not, and what you need from us. Your manager writes two or three lines in PeopleHub after each. Your HR Business Partner checks that all three took place.

## 5. The probation review

The final probation review takes place in **month five**, so that nobody has to rush a decision in the last week. Your manager rates each objective and gives a recommendation. HR and the head of department take the decision. The possible outcomes are **confirmed** or **not confirmed**. If there is a concern, you will hear about it well before the review, not for the first time during it.

## 6. Training in the first 90 days

- HSE induction (ONB-06): mandatory, before site access for site staff.
- Courses assigned in LearnDesk: your manager picks them from your role profile.
- Ask your manager about the learning budget in your first month.

## 7. If something is wrong

Tell your manager first. If you cannot, talk to your HR Business Partner or the HR onboarding desk (hr.onboarding@aldana-group.example, extension 2200). Nobody is penalised for raising a concern in good faith.
